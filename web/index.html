Frequently Asked Questions

Is DocPro secure?

Yes, DocPro uses industry-standard hashing algorithms to generate unique signatures for your documents. These hashes are stored securely and are used to verify the integrity of your files.

Can I use DocPro offline?

Absolutely. DocPro is designed to be offline-first. You can protect and verify documents even without an internet connection. Your operations will be queued and synced with the backend once you're back online.

What file types does DocPro support?

DocPro supports a wide range of file types, including PDF, images (PNG, JPG), and Microsoft Excel and CSV files.
