# ✅ Pricing Accuracy Corrections

## 🚨 **Issue Identified**
The landing page contained misleading statements about the product being "completely free forever" which could damage trust and credibility with potential users.

## 🔧 **Corrections Made**

### **Before (Misleading):**
- "No signup required • Works instantly • **Completely free**"
- "No signup required • Works instantly • **Completely free forever**"

### **After (Accurate):**
- "No signup required • Works instantly • **Try it now**"
- "No signup required • Works instantly • **Start protecting now**"

## ✅ **Statements Kept (Accurate):**
- "Try DocSeal Risk-Free Now" ✓ (Accurate - they can try without risk)
- "You lose nothing" ✓ (Accurate - no upfront commitment)
- "No signup required" ✓ (Accurate - can use immediately)
- "Works instantly" ✓ (Accurate - immediate functionality)
